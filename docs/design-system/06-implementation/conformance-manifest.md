## Completion rule

Stage E may not render its verdict while any row reads PENDING or INCONCLUSIVE. Each such
row must either (a) flip to PASS with evidence cited from this repository on the
then-current tree — fresh evidence, not a stale packet reference — or (b) carry an explicit
accepted-debt annotation pointing at a design-debt-register entry whose "Blocks final
acceptance?" cell says no and whose expiration condition is still live. Rows backed by a
register entry marked as blocking (DD-4, DD-8, DD-10, DD-18r, DD-20, DD-21r and peers)
cannot take the accepted-debt exit; they must be closed. Overclaiming a row is itself a
Stage E defect: any PASS found without reproducible evidence reverts the row and reopens
the gate.
